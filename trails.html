Rock Dunder Nature Reserve

Featured Hiking Trail: Rock Dunder

Rock Dunder Nature Reserve is one of the most scenic hiking trails in Kingston, Ontario. It offers a variety of terrains, including rocky paths, forested areas, and beautiful views of Lake Ontario. The trail is well-marked and suitable for hikers of all skill levels. Visitors can enjoy bird watching, photography, and the peaceful sounds of nature while exploring this hidden gem.
